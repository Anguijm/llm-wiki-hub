## Hypothesis

If we spin up a small set of role-specific Grokbot agents (e.g., COO, CCO, CFO) organized into a group chat with a delegating executive agent, then we can offload strategic planning and cross-functional coordination to an always-on AI leadership team, because Grokbot agents can read each other's outputs, tag each other, and converge on decisions without human orchestration.

## What they did

Nate walked through his live Grokbot setup: a top-level 'Chief' executive agent plus COO, CCO, and CFO agents in a shared 'Leadership' group channel. He prompted them to ideate on Q4 priorities; they deliberated, tagged each other, and produced five agreed decisions. He also demonstrated a hierarchy where each specialized agent (content, X-monitoring, Fireflies, etc.) can be delegated to by the leadership layer. He framed the AI Operating System as: Context, Connections, Capabilities, Cadence.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly maps to the orchestration layer of our dev loop—replacing ad-hoc LLM calls with a persistent, role-separated multi-agent team that can run planning, review, and delegation steps asynchronously while the human focuses on high-leverage work.
