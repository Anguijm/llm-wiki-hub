## Hypothesis

If we map our team's AI-assisted dev workflow and identify where human review or approval gates cluster, then we can restructure handoffs to increase throughput because the bottleneck is rarely the model—it's the human queue downstream.

## What they did

Nate diagnosed why teams using AI tools still fail to ship faster, pointing to process and review bottlenecks rather than model capability as the limiting factor, and proposed a structural fix to team workflow around AI output.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly relevant: the YOLO loop may stall at human review of agent output. Mapping where we gate on human judgment and automating or parallelizing those gates could unlock shipping velocity.
