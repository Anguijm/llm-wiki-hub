## Hypothesis

If we wire Claude to a video editing tool suite via function calling, then it can orchestrate complex multi-step video edits from natural language instructions because Claude's instruction-following and sequencing capabilities are now strong enough to manage non-linear editing workflows.

## What they did

Speaker demonstrated Claude controlling video editing software or pipelines through agentic tool use, producing edits that previously required manual timeline work, showing dramatically improved capability over prior attempts.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Tests the boundary of the YOLO loop's agentic reach into creative/media tooling — relevant if the loop needs to produce or modify video artifacts as part of a pipeline output.
