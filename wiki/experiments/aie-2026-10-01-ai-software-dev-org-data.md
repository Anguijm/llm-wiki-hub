## Hypothesis

If we compare our current AI tooling choices and workflow patterns against the empirical data from 400+ organizations, then we will identify blind spots or underutilized practices that correlate with higher developer productivity because large-scale observational data reveals signal that individual intuition misses.

## What they did

Justin Reock from DX presented survey and telemetry data collected from over 400 organizations on how software development teams are actually adopting and using AI tools, including what is working, what is not, and where the gaps are.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Understanding which AI dev practices correlate with productivity gains at scale can validate or challenge our current YOLO loop design choices around tool selection, review cadence, and agent autonomy.
