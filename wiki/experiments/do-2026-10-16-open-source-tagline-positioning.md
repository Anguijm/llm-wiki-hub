# Reframe a project's tagline as a clear open-source alternative to a known tool

> Back to [[experiments-index]]

## Hypothesis

If we reframe a project's positioning as 'the open-source alternative to X', then adoption and discoverability will improve because users instantly understand the value proposition without needing to learn a new mental model.

## What they did

Supabase co-founder described how changing their tagline to 'the open-source Firebase alternative' was the single turning point that made developers immediately understand why they'd want to try it; prior to that, despite having a great product, the communication wasn't landing.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

medium: applies to how we frame and document agent harnesses and tools so other developers (and agents) immediately understand their purpose.
