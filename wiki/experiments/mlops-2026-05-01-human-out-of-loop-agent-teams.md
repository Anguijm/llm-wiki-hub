## Hypothesis

If we define explicit handoff contracts and shared context schemas between agents in a multi-agent pipeline, then we can reduce human approval checkpoints by at least 50% because agents will have the structured information they need to make decisions autonomously.

## What they did

The speaker presented patterns and architectures for orchestrating teams of AI agents with minimal human intervention, covering how to structure agent roles, communication, and escalation paths so humans are only involved at true decision boundaries.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

The YOLO loop aspires to autonomous end-to-end execution. Reducing human-in-the-loop interrupts is the core thesis. This talk directly provides architectural patterns for achieving that across multi-agent workflows.
