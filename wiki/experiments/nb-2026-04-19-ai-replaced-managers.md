## Hypothesis

If we replace human coordination/management checkpoints in a multi-agent dev loop with an AI orchestrator, then we reduce iteration latency and overhead because AI can route tasks, resolve blockers, and track state faster than async human review.

## What they did

Discussed Jack Dorsey's claim that AI replaced 4,000 managers at Block by handling task routing, status tracking, and decision arbitration previously done by middle management.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly maps to the orchestration layer of the YOLO loop — if human review gates between agent steps can be replaced by an AI coordinator, loop throughput increases significantly.
