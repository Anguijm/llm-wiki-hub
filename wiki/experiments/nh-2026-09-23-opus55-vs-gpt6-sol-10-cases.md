## Hypothesis

If we compare Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol on 10 real-world use cases, then we can determine which model is better suited for reasoning-heavy or efficiency-sensitive workloads because Sol likely represents a different capability/cost tradeoff than Astra.

## What they did

Nate ran Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol through 10 real-world use cases side-by-side, evaluating output quality per task to determine relative strengths.
