## Hypothesis

If we treat the agent feedback loop as the primary product artifact rather than individual outputs, then we create compounding value over time because each loop iteration improves the system and the accumulated loop state is more valuable than any single output it produces.

## What they did

Roland Gavrilescu from Introspection presents a product philosophy and architecture where the continuous improvement loop — not any individual feature or output — is what gets shipped and maintained. He likely covers how to instrument, version, and improve the loop itself as a first-class engineering artifact.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Philosophically and architecturally central to the YOLO loop project — validates the core premise and may offer concrete architectural patterns for treating the loop as a versioned, improvable system.
