## Hypothesis

If we give a multimodal frontier model (GPT-6 Astra) access to real-time data tools and autonomous execution capability in a high-stakes domain, then we can measure how far agentic decision-making has matured because financial outcomes provide unambiguous, quantifiable feedback loops.

## What they did

Nate allocated $10,000 of real capital to GPT-6 Astra and let it autonomously make stock trading decisions, documenting the decisions, reasoning, and financial results over the experiment period.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Tests the full agentic loop end-to-end: tool use, real-world feedback, and autonomous decision chaining. Relevant to how we design action execution and rollback safeguards in the YOLO loop.
