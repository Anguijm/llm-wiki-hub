## Hypothesis

If we substitute GLM-5.2 for a closed frontier model in our coding agent pipeline, then we can achieve near-equivalent task performance at lower cost and with local deployment capability because GLM-5.2 claims near-frontier intelligence on open weights.

Zixuan Li from Z.ai presented GLM-5.2, an open-weights model positioning itself at near-frontier capability levels, discussing its architecture, benchmarks, and suitability for agentic coding and reasoning tasks.
