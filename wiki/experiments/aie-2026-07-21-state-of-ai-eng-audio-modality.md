# Add Audio Modality to Existing Agent Pipeline as High-Intent Adoption Signal

**Status:** `backlog` · **Effort:** `medium`

## Hypothesis

If we add an audio input/output interface to our existing text-based agent pipeline, then we will capture a high-intent user segment and differentiate from text-only workflows, because survey data shows 56% of AI engineers not yet using audio plan to adopt it — the highest intent-to-adopt of any modality.

## What they did

Barr Yaron presented results from a 1,048-respondent survey of AI engineers. Audio had the highest intent-to-adopt ratio of any modality (56% of non-users plan to adopt, up from 37% last year). Image generation doubled from 18% to 36% active use, showing what happens when a modality crosses the quality threshold. The survey also showed agents now have 3x more tool/function-call access than last year, and cost has become a first-class constraint appearing in monitoring and architecture decisions.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Expands the YOLO loop's input/output surface — adding audio as a modality for agent interaction could unlock async, hands-free workflows and align with where the engineering community is heading.

## Notes

Secondary finding: 17% of teams have non-developers regularly shipping customer-facing features. Suggests agent tooling should prioritize low-friction interfaces. Full survey report linked in video description.
