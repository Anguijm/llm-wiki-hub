# Use a structured Claude prompt to generate psychologically anchored three-tier pricing

> Back to [[experiments-index]]

## Hypothesis

If we feed Claude a structured prompt specifying our service, target customer, and the manual alternative we replace, then we get defensible three-tier pricing with clear deliverables and value framing because the model can apply psychological anchoring across education/setup, audit/project, and retainer tiers.

## What they did

Nate shares a specific Claude prompt template: describe yourself as a one-person AI consultant offering X service to X customer who currently solves the problem via X manual alternative, then ask Claude to build three pricing tiers with psychological anchoring mapped to (1) education and setup hours, (2) audit or single project, (3) monthly retainer — each with deliverable, price range, and the single most important value communication point.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly applicable as a meta-prompt for pricing any new agent product or consulting engagement we build; tests whether structured prompting produces more defensible output than ad-hoc pricing brainstorms.
