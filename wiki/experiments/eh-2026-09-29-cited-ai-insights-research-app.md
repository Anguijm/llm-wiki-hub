## Hypothesis

If we build or adopt a research tool that surfaces the exact source passage behind every AI-generated claim, then we can dramatically reduce hallucination risk in agent outputs because users can verify grounding at the sentence level rather than trusting aggregate summaries.

## What they did

Echohive showcased a free research app that generates AI insights while simultaneously displaying the precise source text (exact words) that each insight was derived from, creating a fully traceable research workflow.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly relevant to the verification and trust layer of the YOLO loop; implementing passage-level citation in our retrieval-augmented steps would make agent reasoning auditable and catch grounding failures before they propagate.
