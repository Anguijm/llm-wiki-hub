## Hypothesis

If we bridge vision-language models and vision-language-action models into a unified embodied agent loop, then we can achieve robust real-world task execution because VLAs add action-grounding that pure VLMs lack, enabling closed-loop physical or simulated environment control.

## What they did

Armen Aghajanyan from Perceptron AI outlined the architectural and training progression from VLMs to VLAs to fully embodied agents, describing the key design decisions that enable real-world grounding.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

The VLM→VLA→embodied agent progression mirrors how we might evolve our YOLO loop from text-only to multimodal perception to action-taking agents in real or simulated environments.
