# Establish a Public Leadership Contract Before AI Rollout

## Hypothesis

If leaders publicly commit to job-protection intent and articulate clear human roles before rolling out AI tooling, then engineer resistance and sabotage behaviors will decrease because the core fear driving resistance is job insecurity, not technology aversion.

## What they did

Speaker described a three-part framework for overcoming AI resistance: (1) leaders sign a visible 'contract' addressing the elephant in the room—explicitly stating the rollout is not designed to cut existing headcount, and framing growth-rate reduction as the headcount lever instead; (2) scoping the initial change to a bounded pilot with defined parameters; (3) scaling messaging from pilot results to org-wide vision, including safety safeguards and a clear articulation of what humans will own long-term (evals, system design, editorial judgment, strategy alignment). Referenced Jensen Huang's public stance that leaders who cut AI-era employees 'lack imagination.'

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly applicable when onboarding new team members or stakeholders into the YOLO loop—framing AI-assisted dev as role-expanding rather than role-replacing reduces friction when introducing agentic tooling to skeptical engineers.
