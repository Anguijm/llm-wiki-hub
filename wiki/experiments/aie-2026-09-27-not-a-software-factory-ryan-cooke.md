# Identify where the software factory metaphor breaks down in your specific AI dev workflow

> Back to [[experiments-index]]

## Hypothesis

If we critically audit which parts of our AI dev pipeline do NOT fit a factory model—where creative judgment, ambiguity, or tacit knowledge are required—then we can avoid over-automating those stages and achieve better overall system reliability because inappropriate factory thinking causes brittle pipelines.

## What they did

Ryan Cooke from WorkOS pushed back on the software factory framing, arguing that key aspects of professional software development resist factory-style automation and that teams who conflate the two build fragile systems. He outlined where the metaphor fails.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Valuable counter-signal for YOLO loop design: helps identify which loop stages should remain human-in-the-loop and resist full automation, preventing over-confidence in pipeline reliability.
