## Hypothesis

If we pipe a video transcript plus raw footage metadata into Claude and ask it to produce a structured edit decision list, then we can automate the first-pass editing workflow because Claude can identify cuts, pacing, and structure from text alone.

## What they did

Demonstrated using Claude to analyze video content and generate editing instructions, cut lists, or scripts that replace manual video editing tool workflows.
