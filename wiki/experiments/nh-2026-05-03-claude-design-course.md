## Hypothesis

If we redesign YOLO loop system prompts using explicit Claude-specific design principles (role framing, output constraints, chain-of-thought scaffolding), then task accuracy and instruction-following will improve because Claude responds predictably to well-structured prompt architecture.

## What they did

Nate delivered a beginner-to-pro course on designing prompts and interactions specifically for Claude, covering prompt structure, persona setting, output formatting, and escalating complexity patterns.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly applicable to tuning the system prompts and turn structure inside our YOLO loop. Even small prompt design improvements compound across thousands of loop iterations.
