## Hypothesis

If we explicitly map ourselves against the 5-level builder framework (idea passion → customer listening → go-to-market → market thesis → domain-specific AI forecasting), then we can identify the one concrete gap blocking progress because each level has a specific, testable unlock rather than a vague 'build more' directive.

## What they did

Nate described a 5-level AI builder maturity model: Level 1 = passionate about idea only; Level 2 = listens to customers and iterates; Level 3 = understands go-to-market and uses AI to supercharge distribution; Level 4 = has an unfair thesis on the problem space; Level 5 = accurately forecasts domain-specific AI impact to build ahead of the market. He argued that discouragement from OpenAI/Anthropic launches is a symptom of being at Level 1 or 2, and that Level 4-5 builders treat new model releases as tailwinds rather than threats.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

The YOLO loop is a build system; this framework diagnoses whether the bottleneck is product, distribution, thesis, or forecasting. Running this audit before the next sprint prevents optimizing the wrong layer.
