## Hypothesis

If we route image asset creation tasks (mockups, diagrams, UI screenshots) through ChatGPT's image generation instead of human designers or Figma workflows, then we reduce turnaround time and tool-switching in the dev loop because GPT-4o image output is now precise enough for production-adjacent assets.

## What they did

Speaker demonstrated using ChatGPT's native image generation to produce assets that would typically require a graphic designer, social media manager, or marketing person — showing iterative prompting to refine outputs for real team use cases.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly reduces friction in the asset/content generation step of the loop. Can be integrated as a pre-commit or story-card artifact generator, replacing manual handoffs to non-dev team members.
