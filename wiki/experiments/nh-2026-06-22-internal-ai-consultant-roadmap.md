## Hypothesis

If we execute a structured audit-build-pattern-formalize cycle inside an existing organization, then we can generate visible AI ROI and create or claim a formal AI lead role, because organizations are broadly adopting AI but fewer than 6% are executing it well, leaving a documented gap that internal advocates can fill with proof-of-work.

## What they did

The speaker laid out a four-step roadmap for employed professionals to monetize Claude/AI skills without leaving their jobs: (1) Audit — identify one specific workflow bottleneck tied to a KPI; (2) Build — ship a working AI solution for that exact constraint; (3) Pattern recognize — repeat across multiple problems to build an internal track record; (4) Formalize — present the evidence to leadership and propose the AI lead role, or be the obvious candidate when the org is ready. He cited a real case study of a person who went from email developer to Head of AI at a startup in one year by building in public (YouTube, LinkedIn demos) so that when asked 'what have you built?' she could send links.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

The audit step maps directly to YOLO loop instrumentation: identifying which part of our dev loop has the highest latency or error rate and targeting it first with an agent-based fix, then documenting the before/after delta as internal proof.
