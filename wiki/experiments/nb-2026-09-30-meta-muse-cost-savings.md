## Hypothesis

If we delegate a low-interest, repetitive workflow task to an AI agent like Meta's Muse, then it will surface quantifiable cost or time savings we had previously overlooked because AI can analyze patterns across mundane data at scale without fatigue.

## What they did

The creator assigned Meta's Muse AI to handle one of their most tedious recurring jobs and documented what the AI surfaced. The AI identified an annualized savings opportunity of $5,350, suggesting it found inefficiencies or redundancies invisible to the human doing the task manually.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly maps to the audit and retrospective phase of our dev loop — we can run an agent over our own repetitive build tasks, sprint rituals, or infrastructure spend to surface hidden inefficiencies before they compound.
