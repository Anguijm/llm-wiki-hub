## Hypothesis

If we deploy AI agents backed by Firecrawl for web-scale data ingestion and orchestration, then we can build domain-specific intelligence systems (like food supply chain optimization) that go far beyond simple RAG because agents can actively crawl, synthesize, and act on live web data.

## What they did

Cody Menefee from Firecrawl described using AI agents powered by Firecrawl to rebuild food system intelligence — ingesting supply chain data, market data, and agricultural sources at scale to enable agent-driven decisions.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Firecrawl as a live-web data ingestion layer for our agents is directly applicable — our loop could use this pattern to feed agents with fresh external context rather than static knowledge bases.
