## Hypothesis

If we give an AI agent a physical or robotic body with sensorimotor feedback, then its spatial reasoning and real-world task performance improve because embodiment forces grounded representations that disembodied LLMs lack.

## What they did

Cyrus Clarke from MIT Media Lab described giving an AI system a physical body — integrating language model intelligence with robotic/physical hardware — and the emergent behaviors and improved grounding that resulted.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Longer-term signal for our loop — if we evolve toward agents that interact with real environments (browsers, OS, physical sensors), embodiment principles about sensorimotor grounding are directly relevant to agent architecture design.
