## Hypothesis

If we benchmark new models against a fixed set of real personal or team tasks immediately on release rather than relying on public benchmarks, then we will identify the best model for our specific workload faster because public benchmarks don't reflect our actual use patterns.

## What they did

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch described his practice of immediately trying every new model release on a consistent personal task suite to build intuition. He framed this as a way to develop 'taste' for models—knowing which one fits which type of work—rather than deferring to headline benchmark numbers. He noted that this personal benchmarking habit is what separates builders who move fast from those who are paralyzed by model choice.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Applies to the model selection decision point in our dev loop. Creating a small canonical task suite (e.g., five representative coding tasks, two architecture Q&As, one debugging scenario) that we run on every new model release would give us a repeatable, relevant signal for routing decisions.
