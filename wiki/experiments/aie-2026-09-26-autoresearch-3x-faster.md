## Hypothesis

If we implement an autoresearch pipeline that automatically runs experiments, collects results, and feeds findings back into the next iteration, then model development speed will increase significantly (reported 3x) because removing human bottlenecks from the research loop allows continuous iteration.

## What they did

Tejas Bhakta from Morph presents their autoresearch system, which automates the experiment-run-analyze cycle for model development. The system autonomously selects hyperparameters or architectural changes, runs evaluations, interprets results, and queues the next experiment — achieving a reported 3x speedup in model iteration cycles.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly maps to the YOLO loop's core thesis — automates the loop itself, providing a concrete architecture reference for making the loop self-driving rather than human-triggered.
