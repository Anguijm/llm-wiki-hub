# Use Claude's free Docs/Decks feature to auto-generate slide decks from meeting notes

## Hypothesis

If we pipe raw meeting notes into Claude and prompt it to produce a slide deck, then we get a shareable, editable presentation in seconds at no extra cost, because Claude's docs/decks/designs feature is now out of beta and free on every plan, with animated explainers (Motion) available on Team/Enterprise via code-generated MP4.

## What they did

The presenter noted Claude's docs, slide decks, and designs exited beta and are free on all plans. Over 45 million have been created; they are phone-editable. Paid plans also get interactive dashboards with traceable queries. Team/Enterprise get Motion (code-generated animated MP4 explainers). The suggested experiment: paste meeting notes into Claude and say 'Turn these notes into a deck for Monday.' Note: the separate Claude design site closes December 14th.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Our dev loop often produces written artifacts (specs, retros, sprint notes). Automatically converting these to shareable decks or dashboards reduces manual presentation work and keeps stakeholders updated with zero additional tooling cost.
