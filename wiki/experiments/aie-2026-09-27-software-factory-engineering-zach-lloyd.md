## Hypothesis

If we apply factory engineering concepts—throughput, yield rate, defect rate, WIP limits—to our AI dev loop, then we can identify and fix the true bottleneck in our system because manufacturing optimization principles translate directly to agent-based software pipelines.

## What they did

Zach Lloyd, CEO of Warp, argued that software engineering is structurally becoming factory engineering as AI agents parallelize work, and described how to think about and instrument dev pipelines using industrial engineering mental models.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Strategic framing for the YOLO loop: treating the loop as a production pipeline with measurable yield and throughput metrics would let us apply systematic improvement cycles rather than ad-hoc tuning.
