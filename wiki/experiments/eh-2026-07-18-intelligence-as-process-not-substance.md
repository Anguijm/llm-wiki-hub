# Frame AI objectives as pattern-compression processes, not capability lists

## Hypothesis

If we design agent task prompts that frame the agent's role as a pattern-compressing, anticipation-optimizing process rather than a list of capabilities, then task alignment and output coherence will improve because the framing matches the underlying optimization process that transformers actually perform.

## What they did

The video argues that intelligence—biological or machine—is not a substance a being possesses but a process that compresses patterns, anticipates possibilities, and selects what comes next. Machine intelligence is shaped by objectives and data rather than simulating human cognition. The framing suggests treating AI systems on their own terms rather than as human-intelligence simulacra.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly applicable to how we write system prompts and frame agent objectives in the YOLO loop; reframing tasks as compression/anticipation problems rather than human-mimicry tasks could improve agent self-direction.
