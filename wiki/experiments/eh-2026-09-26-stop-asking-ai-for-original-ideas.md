# Prompt AI to Recombine Existing Ideas Instead of Generating Novel Ones

## Hypothesis

If we instruct AI to recombine, remix, or synthesize existing known ideas rather than generate 'original' ones, then output quality and usefulness will increase because AI models are fundamentally interpolation engines trained on existing human knowledge rather than true creative generators.

## What they did

The video argues that prompting AI for 'original' ideas is a category error — AI excels at recombining and remixing existing concepts. The presenter likely demonstrates reframing prompts away from 'give me an original idea' toward 'combine X with Y in context Z' to get higher-quality, more actionable outputs.

## Relevance to YOLO loop

Directly applies to how we prompt during ideation phases of the YOLO loop — reframing prompts for the spec-writing or brainstorming steps to get better signal from the model.
